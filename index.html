Ustaad — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Ustaad ("we", "our", or "us") is an exam preparation app for Pakistani university students. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Full name and email address (when you register)
  • Google account information (if you sign in with Google)
  • Quiz results and progress data
  • Selected exam type and degree program

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To track your study progress and streaks
  • To personalise your exam preparation experience
  • To send important app notifications

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase, hosted on servers in Mumbai, India (asia-south1 region). We do not sell your data to any third party.

Third Party Services

Ustaad uses the following third party services:

  • Google Firebase — authentication and database
  • Google Sign-In — optional social login
  • Google Gemini API — AI-powered explanations

Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below. We will process your request within 7 days.

Children's Privacy

Ustaad is intended for students aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
